About Me

Yuna Ye

I’m a web developer and graphic designer from Brooklyn, New York, currently earning my associate degree in Multimedia Programming and Design at Borough of Manhattan Community College (BMCC), part of CUNY. I am especially interested in creating alternate reality game (ARG) websites that combine web design, puzzles, codes, and interactive storytelling. I enjoy horror as a genre and am interested in creating online experiences where the viewer becomes an active participant, discovering clues and gradually uncovering a larger story. My process begins with developing the concept and storyline, brainstorming puzzles and interactions, and planning how a user will move through the experience. From there, I design the visual system and website layout and use my coding skills to build the project. Through this work, I am developing skills in web development, graphic design, interaction design, and storytelling. I hope to continue exploring interactive media and creating digital experiences that are both visually engaging and fun to investigate..
